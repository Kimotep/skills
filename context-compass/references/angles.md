# Angles library — lenses for "Open it up"

These are starting points for Step 3 of [`SKILL.md`](../SKILL.md). For each task type, a
short list of lenses — things people often haven't decided yet. Pick the 2–3 that seem most
relevant to what the user described in Step 0. Don't present more than that, and don't force
a lens that clearly doesn't apply.

Each lens should be phrased as a real, answerable choice — not an abstract question.

---

## Build / make something

- **Scope** — a smaller first version, or the full thing from the start?
- **Lifespan** — one-off, or something you'll maintain and come back to?
- **Reuse** — build from scratch, or adapt/extend something that already exists?
- **Audience** — just for you, or will others use or see it?

## Write something

- **Audience** — who reads this, and what do they need to walk away with?
- **Length/polish** — short and casual, or longer and more refined?
- **Reuse** — a one-off piece, or something that becomes a template for more?

## Research / decide

- **Depth** — a quick scan to get oriented, or a thorough comparison before deciding?
- **Bias check** — is there an option already favored that's worth stress-testing rather than
  confirming?
- **Output** — a straight recommendation, or the options laid out for you to choose?

## Fix / debug

- **Scope** — just this one instance, or is it worth checking for the same issue elsewhere?
- **Urgency** — a quick patch now, or worth doing properly even if slower?
- **Side effects** — anything else that depends on the thing being fixed, that should be
  checked afterward?

## Plan / organize

- **Horizon** — just the next step, or the fuller plan/roadmap?
- **Ownership** — just you, or does part of this get handed to someone/something else?
- **Constraints** — is there a deadline or limit that should shape this from the start?

---

## General-purpose fallback lenses

Use these when the task doesn't fit neatly into one category above, or as a second angle
alongside a category-specific one.

- **Reversibility** — is this easy to undo or change later, or more of a one-way decision?
- **Timing** — does this need to happen now, or could it wait or be scheduled?
- **Definition of done** — how will it be obvious this is finished, vs. still in progress?
